About

Few Square Feet publishes short buying guides for people living in small spaces: studios, rentals, first apartments and shared flats.

How we choose

We look for pieces that save floor space, work in a rental, and have a shape or color worth looking at. We read product specifications and customer feedback, and we write each note ourselves. We have not personally tested every product, and we say so here because you should know. Check dimensions and recent reviews on the listing before you buy.

How we make money

Links to products are affiliate links. As an Amazon Associate I earn from qualifying purchases. That does not change the price you pay.
